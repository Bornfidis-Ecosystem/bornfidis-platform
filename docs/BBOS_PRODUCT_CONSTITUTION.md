# BBOS Product Constitution (V1)

**Status:** Canonical, executive-approved
**Governs:** every BBOS asset — Main Guide, workbooks, calculators, templates, checklists, and the AI Prompt Pack
**Companion document:** `docs/BBOS_EDITORIAL_STYLE_GUIDE.md`

This Constitution records the approved product decisions for Bornfidis Business Operating System — Version 1. It is the single source of truth for what BBOS is, who it serves, what it promises, and where its boundaries are. It introduces no new strategy; it fixes decisions already approved.

---

## 1. Identity

- **Product:** Bornfidis Business Operating System — Version 1
- **Abbreviation:** BBOS (all caps)
- **Product format:** a downloadable package — Main Guide, workbooks, calculators, templates, checklists, and an AI Prompt Pack (plain files: guide + spreadsheets + documents). No app, portal, login, or subscription.

## 2. Audience

Service-business owners in their first three years of operation who already have customers or early revenue but lack consistent systems: dependable pricing, organized follow-up, written processes, and clear financial visibility.

## 3. Promise (canonical)

> BBOS helps small service-business owners replace chaotic daily operations with a clear weekly rhythm, confident pricing, a visible sales pipeline, reusable systems, and practical financial control within 90 days.

The promise is about **installed habits and systems the owner controls** — never about specific income, revenue, or profit results.

## 4. Principles (fixed order and names)

These six Bornfidis decision filters govern every framework, template, and checklist. Each is enforced by tools, not slogans.

1. **Integrity** — do what you said; price honestly; keep records straight.
2. **Excellence** — ship to a defined standard, not to mood or time pressure.
3. **Profitability** — price to a target margin and protect it deliberately.
4. **Sustainability** — build a pace and cash position that can last.
5. **Service** — solve the customer's real problem; follow up; recover well.
6. **Legacy** — make the business bigger than the owner through documented systems.

## 5. Differentiator

Practical systems informed by real operating experience, refined for a global small-business audience. BBOS is a coherent operating system bound by a 90-day journey and shared principles — not a loose collection of files.

## 6. Boundaries (what BBOS is NOT)

- Not an MBA textbook.
- Not motivational self-help.
- Not legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice.
- Not a generic template bundle.
- Not a video course or LMS.
- Not an income guarantee.
- Not an AI subscription product.

## 7. Canonical Eight-Module Structure

| # | Module | Core outcome |
|---|--------|--------------|
| 1 | **Business Foundation** | The owner can state their core offer, ideal customer, and current operating reality on one page. |
| 2 | **Offer and Revenue Engine** | A structured offer and a visible pipeline with a follow-up cadence. |
| 3 | **Pricing With Dignity** | A documented, margin-aware pricing method the owner can defend. |
| 4 | **Weekly Operating Rhythm** | A repeatable weekly cadence: plan, do, follow-up, close. |
| 5 | **Money and Metrics** | Weekly review of a small Core Number Set (revenue, margin, cash, runway). |
| 6 | **Systems and Delegation** | Core processes documented as SOPs; at least one handed off. |
| 7 | **Reputation and Customer Retention** | A repeatable post-delivery, referral, and reactivation cadence. |
| 8 | **The 90-Day Implementation Plan** | The 13-week installation of everything above, with evidence. |

## 9. Scope Control for V1

**Required for V1:** Main Guide (all 8 modules); Start Here guide; Weekly Operating Rhythm workbook; Quarterly Planning workbook; Pricing and Margin calculator; Cash-Flow and Runway calculator; SOP template; Client proposal template; Offer One-Pager; Weekly Close checklist; BBOS AI Prompt Pack; one consistent professional delivery package.

**Valuable after launch:** industry-specific worked examples; a filled "gold standard" sample set; editable formats + light onboarding sequence; per-tool walkthroughs; currency/localization variants; an optional guided 90-day companion.

**Excluded from V1:** video course; LMS; certification; community; membership; mobile app; AI subscription; complex dashboard; advanced accounting; legal or tax advice; and anything that reintroduces these (portals, logins, automated integrations).

## 10. Confidentiality & Promise Discipline (binding)

- No client names, private financial data, credentials, or internal operational specifics anywhere in BBOS.
- No real Bornfidis, VelocityMaid, or customer data copied into content or examples.
- No income, revenue, profit, or "results" guarantees.
- No legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice; a plain non-advice note appears wherever money topics arise.
- Real internal material may inform content only after being generalized and stripped of identifiers.

## 11. Canonical Running Example — Maya's Property Services

BBOS uses **one fictional business** consistently across all modules and assets so the system visibly compounds. All figures are **fictional, internally consistent, and clearly labeled as examples**. No example financial figure is ever presented as a promise or expected result.

**Profile**
- **Business:** Maya's Property Services (fictional)
- **Owner:** Maya
- **Business age:** 18 months
- **Team:** Maya plus one part-time worker
- **Services:** residential cleaning, turnover cleaning (short-term rentals), and light property support
- **Customers:** homeowners, short-term rental hosts, and small property managers

**Current condition (Day 0)**
- Inconsistent pricing (quotes vary by mood and by client pressure)
- Scattered follow-ups (leads and past clients go cold)
- No written SOPs (everything lives in Maya's head)
- Limited cash-flow visibility (no reliable view of margin, cash, or runway)
- Too much knowledge held by the owner (Maya is the bottleneck)

**Usage rules**
- Reuse the **same name and the same numbers** across modules; when a figure evolves, show the change explicitly.
- Always label as *Example* and keep figures round and obviously illustrative.
- Never derive Maya's numbers from real Bornfidis/VelocityMaid pricing or any client record.
- Never state or imply an income outcome ("Maya earned $X"); show **process and decisions**, not earnings.
