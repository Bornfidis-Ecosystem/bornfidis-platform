**Status:** Architecture approved. This is a design/production blueprint only — **not** Module 4 prose.  
**Canonical title:** **Weekly Operating Rhythm**

**Design authority:** Constitution §7 (M4), §8 (Week 2 install; Weeks 4/8/12 consolidation), §9 (Weekly Operating Rhythm workbook; Weekly Close checklist).  
**Locked inputs:** Modules 1–3; Pricing & Margin Calculator Frozen V1.0. Do not modify those assets in this pass.

## 1. Purpose

Turn the decisions and tools from Modules 1–3 into a repeatable working week. Move the owner from reactive daily activity to a clear operating cycle that fits someone still actively delivering customer work.

## 2. Customer transformation

- **Starting condition:** The week is controlled by whoever calls first. Snapshot, pipeline, prices, and scripts exist but are not used on a cadence.
- **Exit condition:** A written weekly rhythm with scheduled Planning and Pipeline/Follow-Up blocks, 2–3 Daily Anchors, a delivery-quality routine, a completed Weekly Close with Next-Week Handoff, a Catch-Up Protocol, and 4–6 Rhythm Protection Rules.
- **Behavioral shift:** From “survive the week” to “run the week.”
- **Does not promise:** More revenue, fewer emergencies, or a perfect calendar. No income guarantees.

## 3. Approved cycle mapping

| Cycle step | Operating meaning |
|---|---|
| Plan | Weekly Planning Block |
| Deliver | Delivery work + Daily Anchors + Delivery Quality |
| Sell and Follow Up | Pipeline and Follow-Up Block |
| Review | Light Money and Pricing Checkpoint (inside Sell block) |
| Close | The Weekly Close + brief Next-Week Handoff |

## 6. Time-block governance (approved)

| Block | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly Planning Block | **25–40 min** (target ~**30**) | No universal day/time |
| Pipeline and Follow-Up Block | **30–60 min** (target ~**45**) | Includes light Money and Pricing Checkpoint |
| The Weekly Close | **25–45 min** (hard ceiling **45** normal week; stretch to **60** only in consolidation/catch-up weeks) | Ends with brief Next-Week Handoff |

## 7. Next-Week Handoff (required Close section)

Brief fields only — must not become next week’s Planning Block:

1. Next week’s top priority  
2. First unresolved follow-up  
3. First delivery risk or capacity concern  
4. Date/window of the next Weekly Planning Block
